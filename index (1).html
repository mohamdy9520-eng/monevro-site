Monevro

A personal finance app that reads your receipts, tracks your spending, and helps you reach your savings goals.

Get it on Google Play

What you can do with Monevro

Scan receipts with AI
Take a photo of a receipt. Monevro extracts the amount, merchant, and category, including receipts in Arabic.
Get AI-generated plans
Monevro analyzes your spending and suggests a plan based on your own data.
Set budgets and goals
Follow your income and expenses by category and watch your progress toward each goal.
See your statistics
Charts show where your money goes each month.
Use your own currency
Pick from many currencies. The app works in English and Arabic.
Keep your account secure
Sign in with email or Google, and lock the app with your fingerprint or face.
